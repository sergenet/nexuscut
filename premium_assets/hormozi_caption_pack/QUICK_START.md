# Quick Start: Hormozi Bold Glow

1. In NexusCut, generate captions for your video.
2. Import `Hormozi_Bold_Glow.nexuscut.json` in **Caption Styles**.
3. Apply the style to all captions.
4. Highlight one to three key words per sentence in yellow: numbers, outcomes, objections, and verbs.

## Best results

- Keep each caption to 2-5 words.
- Use a high-contrast talking-head shot.
- Leave the bottom 25% of the frame clear for captions.
- Use pop animation sparingly; let the words carry the energy.

## Example

`YOU DON'T NEED MORE TIME` -> highlight **MORE TIME** in yellow.
