# 10 Proven Viral Hooks to Stop the Scroll

1. **The 'Controversial' Hook**
   "Stop doing [Common Practice] if you want to [Desired Result]. Do this instead."
   *Example: "Stop running Facebook ads if you want to scale your agency. Do this instead."*

2. **The 'Secret' Hook**
   "This one [Tool/Strategy] changed my entire [Industry/Life], and nobody is talking about it."
   *Example: "This one AI video editor changed my entire content pipeline, and nobody is talking about it."*

3. **The 'Mistake' Hook**
   "99% of people fail at [Goal] because they make this one stupid mistake."
   *Example: "99% of creators fail at YouTube Shorts because they make this one stupid mistake."*

4. **The 'Story/Journey' Hook**
   "How I went from [Negative State] to [Positive State] in just [Timeframe]."
   *Example: "How I went from 0 to 100k followers in just 30 days without spending a dime."*

5. **The 'Time-Saver' Hook**
   "If you don't have time to [Task], here is exactly how you can do it in 5 minutes."
   *Example: "If you don't have time to edit videos, here is exactly how you can do it in 5 minutes."*

6. **The 'Listicle' Hook**
   "Here are the top [Number] [Things] you need to [Achieve Goal] right now."
   *Example: "Here are the top 3 AI tools you need to explode your reach right now."*

7. **The 'Myth Buster' Hook**
   "Everything you've been told about [Topic] is a complete lie."
   *Example: "Everything you've been told about the TikTok algorithm is a complete lie."*

8. **The 'Curiosity Gap' Hook**
   "I just found a loophole that lets you [Incredible Benefit]. Let me show you."
   *Example: "I just found a loophole that lets you bypass the 200-view jail. Let me show you."*

9. **The 'Specific Audience' Hook**
   "If you are a [Target Audience] struggling with [Pain Point], you need to watch this."
   *Example: "If you are a real estate agent struggling with getting leads, you need to watch this."*

10. **The 'Proof' Hook**
    "I tried the [Famous Person/Brand] strategy for [Timeframe]. Here is exactly what happened."
    *Example: "I tried the Alex Hormozi content strategy for 30 days. Here is exactly what happened."*
