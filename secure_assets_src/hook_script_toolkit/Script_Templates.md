# Fill-in-the-Blank Script Templates

## 1. The Educational / Talking-Head Script

**Hook:** "Stop doing [Common Practice] if you want to [Desired Result]. Here is the 3-step framework I use instead."
**Intro:** "Most people think the secret to [Desired Result] is [Common Misconception]. But that's exactly why they stay stuck at [Current Frustrating Level]."
**Body 1:** "Step 1: You need to eliminate [Pain Point Source]. When you do this, you free up [Resource - Time/Money]."
**Body 2:** "Step 2: Start doing [New Strategy] for just [Timeframe] every day. This creates [Benefit]."
**Body 3:** "Step 3: Leverage [Tool/Asset] to scale the whole process without extra work."
**CTA:** "If you want the exact checklist I use for this, just comment [Keyword] below and I'll DM it to you for free."

## 2. The Reaction / Commentary Script

**Hook:** "I literally cannot believe [Creator/Brand] just did this. Let's break it down."
**Context:** "So [Creator] just posted a video claiming that [Controversial Statement]. At first glance, it sounds crazy."
**Reaction/Analysis:** "But if you look closely at [Specific Detail], you'll notice that they are actually using the [Strategy Name] strategy. This works incredibly well because [Psychological Reason]."
**Takeaway:** "The lesson here for [Your Audience] is that you don't need [Complicated Thing]. You just need [Simple Concept]."
**CTA:** "Do you agree with [Creator]? Let me know in the comments."

## 3. The Storytelling / Journey Script

**Hook:** "Exactly [Timeframe] ago, I had [Negative Situation]. Today, I have [Massive Success]. Here is the brutal truth of how I did it."
**The Low Point:** "I remember sitting in my [Location], completely stressed because [Specific Problem]. I tried [Failed Attempt 1] and [Failed Attempt 2]. Nothing worked."
**The Turning Point:** "Then, I stumbled across a weird concept called [Concept Name]. I decided I had nothing to lose."
**The Transformation:** "I applied it by doing [Action 1] and [Action 2]. Within [Short Timeframe], I started seeing [Early Result]. Fast forward to today, and it completely changed my [Area of Life]."
**CTA:** "If you're currently stuck where I was, don't give up. Follow me for more behind-the-scenes on how to master [Topic]."
