# Short-Form Video Script Templates
*Brought to you by NexusCut*

Don't just wing it when you hit record. Use these fill-in-the-blank script frameworks to structure your videos for maximum watch time.

## 1. The Educational Loop
*Best for: Tutorials, Tips, "How-to" videos.*

**[0:00 - 0:03] The Hook**
State the problem or the promise immediately.
> "Stop doing [Bad Habit] if you want to [Desired Result]."

**[0:03 - 0:15] The Context / Empathy**
Show them you understand the pain point.
> "For months, I struggled with [Pain Point], and I almost gave up. Then I discovered this one trick."

**[0:15 - 0:45] The Value Delivery (Step-by-Step)**
Give actionable advice. Use fast cuts here.
> "Step 1: [Action]. 
> Step 2: [Action]. 
> Step 3: [Action]."

**[0:45 - 0:60] The Call to Action + Loop**
Tell them what to do next, and seamlessly transition into the hook.
> "Save this video for later, because you need to... [Loop back to Hook: 'Stop doing Bad Habit...']"

## 2. The Reaction / Storytelling Framework
*Best for: Vlogs, Storytimes, Reacting to crazy news.*

**[0:00 - 0:03] The Visual Hook**
Show the craziest part of the story visually before explaining it.
> *(On screen: You looking shocked at an article/video)* "I cannot believe [Subject] actually did this."

**[0:03 - 0:20] The Build Up**
Explain how the situation started.
> "So it all started when [Event happened]. Everyone thought it was normal, until..."

**[0:20 - 0:40] The Climax (The Twist)**
The craziest part of the story.
> "...[The Twist]. Literally nobody saw this coming."

**[0:40 - 0:50] The Takeaway / Question**
End on a cliffhanger or a question to drive comments.
> "What would you have done in this situation? Let me know below."

---

## 3. The Listicle
*Best for: Tool recommendations, favorite products, top 3s.*

**[0:00 - 0:03] The Blanket Statement**
> "Here are the top 3 [Items] that completely changed my [Area of Life]."

**[0:03 - 0:15] Item 1**
> "Number 1 is [Item]. I use this every single day for [Benefit]."

**[0:15 - 0:30] Item 2**
> "Number 2 is [Item]. If you struggle with [Problem], this is the fix."

**[0:30 - 0:45] Item 3 (The Best One)**
> "But number 3 is my absolute favorite. It's [Item], and it allows you to [Huge Benefit]."

**[0:45 - 0:55] CTA**
> "Link in my bio to get all of these. Follow for more!"
