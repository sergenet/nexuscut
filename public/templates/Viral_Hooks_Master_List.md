# The Viral Hooks Master List
*Brought to you by NexusCut*

You have exactly 3 seconds to convince a viewer not to swipe away. Use these 10 proven frameworks for your next short-form video:

### 1. The Negative Frame
*Why it works:* People are hardwired to avoid mistakes. Negativity bias grabs attention faster than positive framing.
*Formula:* "You are making this massive mistake with [Topic]."
*Example:* "You are making this massive mistake with your TikTok edits."

### 2. The Secret Intel
*Why it works:* People want insider knowledge that gives them an unfair advantage.
*Formula:* "The secret to [Topic] that nobody talks about."
*Example:* "The secret to 10k followers that nobody talks about."

### 3. The "Illegal" Trick
*Why it works:* Taboo subjects or "forbidden" knowledge creates intense curiosity.
*Formula:* "This [Niche] trick feels illegal to know."
*Example:* "This video editing trick feels illegal to know."

### 4. The Authority Challenge
*Why it works:* It positions you as an expert who knows better than the mainstream.
*Formula:* "Stop doing [Common Action]. Do this instead."
*Example:* "Stop typing your captions manually. Do this instead."

### 5. The Blueprint
*Why it works:* High perceived value. It promises a structured, easy-to-follow guide.
*Formula:* "Here is the exact step-by-step blueprint I used to [Result]."
*Example:* "Here is the exact step-by-step blueprint I used to go viral."

### 6. The Timeline Result
*Why it works:* It promises a specific outcome in a specific timeframe.
*Formula:* "How I achieved [Result] in just [Timeframe]."
*Example:* "How I gained 5,000 subscribers in just one week."

### 7. The Direct Call-Out
*Why it works:* Highly targeted. If the viewer fits the description, they *must* watch.
*Formula:* "If you are a [Target Audience], you need to watch this."
*Example:* "If you are a content creator, you need to watch this."

### 8. The "I Tried It All"
*Why it works:* Saves the viewer time. You did the hard work for them.
*Formula:* "I tested every [Topic] hack, and only ONE actually works."
*Example:* "I tested every AI video editor, and only ONE actually works."

### 9. The Myth Buster
*Why it works:* Challenges a widely held belief, creating immediate intrigue.
*Formula:* "Everything you know about [Topic] is a lie."
*Example:* "Everything you know about the TikTok algorithm is a lie."

### 10. The Regret Hook
*Why it works:* FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).
*Formula:* "I wish I knew this before I started [Action]."
*Example:* "I wish I knew this before I started making YouTube Shorts."
